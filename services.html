What does the transfer price cover?

Standard costs $25 per one-way trip. Premium costs $45 per round trip, including the return journey. VIP transfer is included at $0. Choose 1–30 trips in the estimator; transfer quantities carry into the booking.

How are rental days calculated?

Daily rental rates are $35 Standard, $70 Premium, and $120 VIP. In a booking, rental days match the nights between check-in and check-out. A three-night Standard rental adds $105.

How much is a tour for my group?

The Standard group tour is $20 per person, so two guests add $40. Premium includes a private guide for $50 per party; the VIP special tour costs $100 per party. These private-party prices are charged once, not multiplied by guests.

Can I book a service on its own?

Travel extras are part of a hotel booking in this project. Choose one service with your stay, or continue without an extra. All reservations are stored only in your browser.
