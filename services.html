What does the transfer price cover?

The $35 flat fee represents one arrival transfer from the airport to the selected hotel. It is added once to the demo booking, regardless of the number of nights.

How are rental days calculated?

Car rental costs $55 per day. In a booking, the number of rental days matches the number of nights between check-in and check-out. For example, a three-night stay adds $165.

How much is a tour for my group?

The guided city tour costs $45 per person. A booking for two guests adds $90. The estimator lets you compare a different group size before continuing.

Can I book a service on its own?

Travel extras are part of a hotel booking in this demo. Choose one service with your stay, or continue without an extra. All reservations are stored only in your browser.
