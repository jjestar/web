Is this a real booking service?

BookingApp is a student travel project. The stays and prices are illustrative. You can explore, create a local demo account and save a demo trip, but no real reservation or payment is made.

Where are my saved stays and trips?

Log in and open My trips to see your saved stays and demo bookings. Your data stays in this browser. Clearing browser storage removes it; it does not sync between devices.

Can I cancel a demo booking?

Yes. Open your profile, find the trip and choose Cancel booking. You will be asked to confirm before its status changes.
