    How does a demo booking work?

    Choose your dates and room, add your guest details, and save your plan to your profile. Nothing is sent to a hotel and no payment is taken.

    Can I change my mind?

    You can cancel a saved demo booking from your profile, then create another with your new dates. Your plans stay in this browser.

    Are these real hotel listings?

    These are illustrative stays built for our student project. Names, prices, amenities, and photos demonstrate the booking experience; they are not live availability.
