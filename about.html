About BookingApp

A student-built travel website for comparing hotels and planning a trip in one place.

Who it is for

Compare before you choose

BookingApp is for travelers who want to compare destinations, amenities, and nightly prices in one clear view. Save a shortlist, check the local weather, and plan a stay with the details that matter to you.

Created as a student team project, this website uses illustrative hotel listings and local booking simulations. It does not make real reservations or collect payments.
